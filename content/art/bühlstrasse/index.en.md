I had never painted a night scene, so one evening I went out to try —
no further than Bühlstrasse, the street crossing around the corner
from my apartment in Bern.

Bern’s streetlights today are plain white LEDs — nothing like this.
Before painting, I tinted the photo yellow myself, to fake the warm
sodium-vapor glow streetlights like these gave off back in the 1970s.
I just liked the effect: everything under it turns a strange,
saturated orange, headlights blur into streaks, a shopfront dissolves
into shadow, a passerby crosses right through the middle of it. That
color is really what the painting is about — even if the street
doesn’t actually look like that anymore.
