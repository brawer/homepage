I had never painted a night scene, so one evening I went out to try,
right around the corner from my apartment in Bern, at the intersection
with Bühlstrasse.

Bern’s streetlights today are plain cool-white LEDs. Before
painting, I therefore tinted the photo yellow myself to mimic the warm
light of the sodium-vapor lamps that were common in the 1970s. I
just liked the effect: everything appears in a strangely saturated
orange; headlights blur into streaks; a storefront disappears into the
shadows; a passerby crosses the scene right in the middle of this
light. For me, color is the central element in this painting—even
though the street doesn’t look anything like that anymore.
