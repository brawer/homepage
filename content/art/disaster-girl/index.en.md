Another piece in my [Memes](/tags/memes/) series. [Disaster
Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disaster_Girl) is the 2005 photo
of a young girl smirking in front of a burning house, endlessly
recaptioned to place her calmly watching over one disaster or another
— one of the internet’s most recognizable memes.

I painted this one mainly as an exercise in older oil techniques I
hadn’t tried before — hair, skin tones, and smoke each behave very
differently on canvas, and I wanted to get a feel for all three at
once. The eyes gave me more trouble than I expected, and I can already
see where I’d do them differently now. But I’m genuinely happy with
the skin: building it up through several thin, semi-transparent
layers, the way older paintings often were made, gives it a subtle
shimmer that a single opaque layer just can’t produce. Still plenty to
learn, but this one taught me a lot.
