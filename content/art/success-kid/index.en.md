Another piece in my [Memes](/tags/memes/) series. [Success
Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Success_Kid) started life as a
2007 snapshot of a toddler on a beach, fist clenched, looking
improbably pleased with himself — it went on to caption a decade of
online “mission accomplished” moments.

People react to this painting in two very different ways: some see a
cute, determined little kid; others feel a bit uneasy, reading the
clenched fist as a stand-in for toxic masculinity. I’m firmly in the
second camp. Painting is usually meditative for me — I rarely feel
much of anything while I work. This one was the exception: I had a
genuine, negative reaction to the image, and it stuck with me the
whole time I was painting it.
