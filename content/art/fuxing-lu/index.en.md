Fùxīng Lù is a quiet street in Shanghai’s old [French
Concession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_concession_of_Shanghai),
all plane trees and small shops, with a coffee shop every few doors.
One of them had a big glass front, and the room behind it looked nice,
so I took a photo through the window with my phone and walked on.

I didn’t look at it again for years. When I finally did, going through
old travel pictures, one thing jumped out: the place was full,
everyone packed in around tiny tables — and half of them were staring
at a phone. So was I, technically. That’s how the photo got taken.

That’s what I ended up painting: a warm, crowded room where everyone
is together and nobody is quite there.
