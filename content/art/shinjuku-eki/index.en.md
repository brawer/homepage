On [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/woman-in-white-elbow-sleeved-shirt-standing-near-white-train-in-subway-2TlAsvhqiL0)
I found a freely licensed photograph by [Eutah
Mizushima](https://unsplash.com/@eutahm): a woman waiting on a subway
platform, with a blurred background. It looked like a good
opportunity to learn how to fake motion blur in oil.

I painted with two different whites — zinc white, cool and slightly
gray, for the hazy background, and titanium white, warmed up with a
touch of chromium yellow, for her pullover in the foreground. The
photo was taken at Shinjuku Station in Tokyo, which gave me the
title: “Shinjuku-eki”, Japanese for “Shinjuku Station”.

Once the painting was done, I emailed Eutah a photo of it — a photo
of a painting of a photo, which could in principle go on forever if
either of us felt like it. He wrote back to say he liked it, a
nice way to close the loop: back to where it all started, just with
a lot more oil paint involved.
