This is [Gelateria di Berna](https://gelateriadiberna.ch/), an ice
cream parlor on Mittelstrasse in Bern, a few minutes’ walk from my
apartment. I painted it on a bright summer day — the kind when every
table outside is taken and the line spills onto the sidewalk.

What I find interesting is the wall on the right. In reality it
carries a big piece of graffiti. At first glance it looks quickly
sprayed on, but it is so carefully done that it is almost certainly a
commissioned work. I copied it line for line. In oil — an even slower
medium — it came out a touch more precise than the original itself.
That friction between an everyday format and a gallery medium is the
whole point of my [Memes](/tags/memes/) series — this isn’t a meme,
obviously, but apparently I can’t resist chasing the same clash
everywhere.

The painting outlasted its subject. A few weeks after I finished, the
graffiti was painted over and replaced with a new piece. The first one
now survives only in oil.
