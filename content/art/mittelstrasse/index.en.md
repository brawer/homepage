This is [Gelateria di Berna](https://gelateriadiberna.ch/), an ice
cream parlor on Mittelstrasse in Bern, a few minutes’ walk from my
apartment. I painted it on a bright summer day — the kind when every
table outside is taken and the line spills onto the sidewalk.

What I find interesting is the wall on the right with the big graffiti
that I copied line for line.  In oil — an even slower medium — it came
out a touch more precise than the original itself.  That friction
between an everyday format and a gallery medium is the whole point of
my [Memes](/tags/memes/) series. This isn’t a meme, obviously, but
apparently I can’t resist chasing the same clash everywhere.

The painting outlasted its subject. A few weeks after I finished, the
graffiti was painted over and replaced with a new piece. The first one
now survives only in oil.
