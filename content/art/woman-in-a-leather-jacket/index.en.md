This is the first portrait I painted.  After experimenting with [tools
in oil](/tags/tools/) it dawned on me: people are far more
interesting. I’m fascinated by 1920s art, especially [New
Objectivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Objectivity), and
wanted to try a portrait in that style.

In an exhibition, I had seen [Lady in
Fur](https://lisa.gerda-henkel-stiftung.de/das_schluesselstueck?nav_id=3749&language=en),
a 1925 painting by Wilhelm Lachnit. I didn’t want to paint an exact
copy, though — I wondered instead how she might look today. I don’t
know much about the woman herself, but she was plausibly a
[flapper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flapper), to use the English
term for the Weimar Republic’s modern, liberated women.

She surely wouldn’t wear fur today. And “Lady” — duh. Leather jacket
instead of fur, “Woman” instead of “Lady” — hence the
title “Woman in a Leather Jacket”.
