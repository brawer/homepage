Like everyone else, I spend an embarrassing amount of my life proving
to websites that I’m not a robot. Websites are machines too, but they refuse to send their bytes
to the machine sitting in front of my eyeballs — unless I click on
enough traffic lights first. Normally that’s just background
annoyance. One day, I actually _looked_ at the images — and found
them wonderfully bad: blurry, oddly tilted little photos, snapped, it
turns out, by an actual Street View car having a rough day.

Much like my [Memes](/tags/memes/) series, I got curious what would
happen if I painted these strange, accidental little images in oil.
The result is this 3×3 grid — nine small paintings, each one a tiny
monument to the absurdity of proving you’re human.
