abstract: >
  Extraction-based summaries are only useful if the reader can see how
  they relate to the source. This paper argues that skimmable document
  abstractions need phrasal-level analysis and dynamic,
  context-preserving interfaces. It introduces *temporal typography*,
  in which salient phrases are presented over time, and describes
  several prototype document viewers.
