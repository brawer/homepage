abstract: >
  In slide and graphics software, text usually lives in a fixed-size
  box, and shrinking the box makes the text spill past its edges. This
  technique uses the width axis of a variable font to narrow the
  letterforms as the box gets smaller — and widen them again as it
  grows — so the text keeps fitting its container instead of
  overflowing or being clipped.
