abstract: >
  A workspace tool that speeds up sorting things into categories. As
  you place an item, the system compares it against the groups you have
  already made, highlights the most similar ones, shows which features
  they share, and can scroll them into view — so you classify by
  confirming a suggestion rather than hunting for the right pile.
