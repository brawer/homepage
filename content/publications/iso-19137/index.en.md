abstract: >
  ISO 19107 (Spatial Schema) offers such a rich collection of modeling
  constructs that the same geometry can be described in several ways,
  which impedes interoperability. ISO 19137 defines a small,
  broadly implementable core profile of it — a common subset that any
  community can rely on and then extend for its own needs.

I was the editor and principal author, and took the standard through
ISO/TC 211’s process. It grew out of a background paper I wrote for
Working Group 7 on aligning commonly used spatial formats with
ISO 19107. Available from the
[ISO catalogue](https://www.iso.org/standard/32555.html).
