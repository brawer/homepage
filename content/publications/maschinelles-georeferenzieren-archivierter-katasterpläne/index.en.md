abstract: >
  A pipeline for automatically georeferencing archived Swiss cadastral
  survey plans: OCR and page rasterization, map-symbol recognition, and
  a RANSAC-based fitting procedure that matches recognized survey-point
  symbols against known coordinates to estimate each plan’s affine
  transformation into real-world coordinates. Evaluated on a sample of
  2,551 dossiers from the City of Zürich’s Geomatics and Surveying
  department, which plans to run the pipeline across its full archive —
  an estimated 80,000–100,000 dossiers, still being scanned from paper
  to PDF — once scanning is complete. The talk closes with an outlook
  toward applying the same method to other municipal plan archives.
