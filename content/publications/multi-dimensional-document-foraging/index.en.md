abstract: >
  An interface for exploring a large document collection along several
  structured dimensions at once — author, topic, date, and so on —
  shown side by side. The user repeatedly tightens or loosens the
  query, spiralling in and spiralling out around a visible boundary
  between the documents currently selected and those left out, keeping
  a sense of the whole collection while zeroing in.
