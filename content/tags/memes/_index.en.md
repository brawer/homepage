This is a small series built around one idea: take a format that’s
about as present-day as it gets — the internet meme — and see what
happens when you render it in one of the most traditional mediums
there is, oil on canvas. The format is disposable and instantly
recognizable; the medium is slow and deliberate. I like the friction
between the two.
